## Evaluation interpretation

Freeze the same v2 evidence/rubric/semantic prompts/render policy while substituting a
model. Keep best-qualified configuration comparisons and v1/v2 programme comparisons
separate. Independent source opportunities define recall; model-created inventories are
hypotheses. Conditional repairs and failures are outcomes under the frozen programme.

Reviewer controls must include valid and invalid candidates, with provider identities
hidden and prior source exposure recorded. Model judgments require calibration against
human decisions; this follows [task-specific evaluation guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/evaluation-best-practices).
Actual playback is necessary for publication acceptance. Text-only judgments can assess
selection but cannot establish delivered video quality.
