## How

Uppy 6's plugin reads a `s3Multipart` field on the file (the state Golden Retriever persists) and,
when it finds one, forces multipart and resumes through ListParts. That is the whole trick: the
server creates the multipart as before and knows the UploadId from the first second, and the browser
only has to be told. Everything the S1 client was kept for (server-side truth for resume, the grace
window, the reaper's liveness rule) survives unchanged; what changed is who drives the PUTs.

## Went wrong

- **The research error itself**, and that it survived a day in four places. Reading a doc line about
  form uploads as a statement about signed requests. The rule is in AGENTS.md now.
- **Uppy's uninstall aborts at the store by default.** Found in the source before it bit: navigating
  away inside the app would have thrown the parts away, exactly the legacy's complaint. The client
  refuses to sign the abort while unmounting, and the e2e proves no DELETE reaches the app on leave.
- **Refresh raced the abort.** The first cancel test failed because the row list refreshed on the
  cancel event, before the DELETE had finished. Moved the abort into the app's own handler, awaited,
  then refresh.
- **A local `main` behind `origin/main`** put a docs branch on the wrong base; and a cherry-pick with a
  flag it does not accept ran one gate on an empty branch. Both cheap, both noted.
- **Rajesh merged #18 while its follow-up gate was running**; the push guard from #17 refused the
  stale branch as designed, and the follow-up went out as #19 from main.

### X (shipped, went wrong) · image: the square recomposition of the source page

Day 2 of building Temnia. Sprint one closed last night: a real 2:31 master went in through the browser on staging and came out playable.

What shipped
- upload straight to storage in parts. Close the tab, pick the same file again, it carries on from the parts already stored

- every rendition measured against the original before anything is saved

The numbers
- 1.9 GB up in 12 minutes
- ready in 71 minutes, first attempt after the fix
- 9060.4 s on every rendition against a 9060 s probe
- two thirds of the time is CPU encoding, a third is pushing 13,600 small segments to R2

Too slow at 71 minutes. Sprint two moves the heavy lifting to a GPU.
