## How

Kept source and assignment identities in immutable artifacts. Structured context pages carry
exact JSON fragments; speech fragments preserve the original sentence ID. Admission requires
complete delivered ranges, including evidence retained in verified prior checkpoint segments.
The model receives bounded excerpts rather than a full transcript.

V7 decisions run in Temporal children. An SDK request quantum continues the same assignment from
its checkpoint without consuming an output correction attempt. Exact admitted work is reusable;
unknown paid outcomes retain their fence. Editorial progress is separate from a compiled review
video, so explicit retry can resume review and repair against the expected revision.

## The retried run stopped on `outcome_unknown`; that stop is now settled by the worker

- Run `12b9c920` retried after #51 and stopped at 91 calls with "A provider call ended without a
  confirmed outcome." The ledger: one Gemini review stream dropped after announcing generation
  `gen-1789468147-PmzISSHGfv7FaBYixcdC`; the budgeted model's twenty-second receipt wait found
  nothing; the run was fenced; the one reconciliation at workflow failure skipped the attempt
  because the CLI reconciles only terminal attempts; nothing asked the gateway again. Nineteen
  minutes later the gateway still answered 404 for that generation. A design hole, not a
  provider fact: the ledger's owned recovery path existed and had one test, and no caller.
- On `fix/topics-outcome-unknown`: `receipts.py` drives that recovery from receipts (reported
  charge → settled at the charge; generation unknown to the gateway ten minutes after the stream
  ended → released at zero with the 404 retained; recorded but uncosted → fence kept). The
  decision activity waits on a 30 s to 5 min ladder (17.5 minutes) and continues on its route;
  a sibling decision that meets the run fence waits the same way. The reaper settles fenced V8
  runs whose execution has ended and parks them retryable; live executions are left alone
  (Temporal `describe` is the liveness fact, bound in the worker). The unknown-outcome sentence
  and the panel's Retry refusal now say what happens next.
- Verified against Postgres: reported receipt settles and lifts the fence and a fresh attempt is
  allowed; absent receipt keeps the fence inside the grace then releases at zero; a recorded
  receipt without cost keeps the fence; an attempt without a handle is unresolvable; parking
  needs a clear ledger and the ending execution's identity; the reaper settles a finished run and
  leaves a live one; a decision recovers its own dropped stream through the ledger and finishes
  on the same route; a receipt that never settles is the typed stop after the ladder.
