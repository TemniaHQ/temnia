## How

The whole source is the organizing object. Chapters account for the recording, and deliverables attach through their source ranges. The shared-boundary control changes the end of one chapter and the start of its neighbour together.

Mode switching changes the available tools around the work. The floating glass dock expands its labels, while cut controls and evidence use stable surfaces. Formal approval stays separate from resolving a feedback note.

The prototype uses fictional project data and an original vector illustration for its media frame. No customer footage, customer names, uploads, AI calls, or publishing were used. Its playhead is simulated and its processing/check results are sample states.

## Went wrong

- The first layout put the mode dock below the fold on a laptop-sized viewport. Made the dock persistent and tightened the compact layout; editing content can scroll with bottom clearance.
- Very short chapters made a time ruler imply more precision than the overview layout supported. Labeled the overview's expanded short ranges explicitly; exact ranges remain available in the list and source details.
- The first client preview retained the agency navigation around it. Removed that chrome from the client surface and checked the result at 360px width.
- Caught a stale kept-duration/count readout after restoring the proposed drop. Derived the coverage total and chapter count from the local state.
