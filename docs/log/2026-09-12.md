## What went wrong

Both authors selected the same wealth discussion ending on a fatalistic premise
before the answer. Kimi also separated an opening from its prayer antecedent and
a warning from a later positive qualification. Gemini did not create a prayer
opportunity until its source critic found one; an earlier action/stillness answer
remained without an opportunity. Another developed practice discussion was discovered
but rejected as not contiguously extractable. These different loss points need
different evaluation; counting selected videos does not measure recall.

Individual critics caught concrete defects while source critics sometimes passed
them. In Gemini's run, source review also demanded wealth context over seven minutes
earlier while individual review passed the opening. Its newly discovered prayer
opportunity marked another question as completion. These are source-grounded
calibration concerns, not adjudicated critic error rates.

Kimi's actual patch changed protected opportunity evidence. The validator correctly
rejected the atomic patch, but the prompt asks for updated opportunities and the
schema exposes full records without stating which fields are editable. A pure offline
replay reproduced the refusal; retaining original evidence definitions made the
candidate extensions structurally admissible. That diagnostic copy was never
persisted as a result. The attempted wealth extension would still omit its next
qualification. Separately, the current repair vocabulary cannot directly trim one
candidate or replace both edges in one operation.

Gemini's real repair returned `finish_reason=length`: 32,754 output tokens included
31,454 reasoning tokens against the 32,768 allowance. Its charge settled before the
normalization guard refused truncation. This was not a timeout, unknown expense or
patch-validator rejection. Small exact-schema qualification did not predict whether
this much larger repair request would finish.

## Production-quality continuation

Implemented `standalone-topics/3` from the observed failures instead of continuing
the model comparison on the same defective program. The new workflow inventories
the whole source independently, removes author rationale from portfolio review,
uses a compact source-review contract, supports both-edge extent replacement, gives
repair only finding-authorized source windows, preserves opportunity evidence while
accepting mapping changes, and defaults to three bounded repairs. A strict gate
withholds candidates without an explicit source selection or with a required
finding.

The completed Karma run `0c3a9707-30b9-558f-b0e3-1098bd1c04e2` used Kimi K3 as
author and Gemini 3.8 Flash as independent inventory/reviewer through OpenRouter.
It spent $0.652119 across 18 attempts and rendered ten MP4/VTT pairs. Initial review
found incomplete karma-fundamentals and wealth endings, a missing three-strands
opening and unsafe physical edges. One live repair was accepted, the changed
candidates received fresh cold review, and a fresh source review completed.

s349–s356 after its topic ended at s348. Direct transcript inspection showed that

publication correction. Replaying the immutable r11 selection and review under that

286,069,192 bytes.
