# Building Temnia in public

**Status:** v2.0, 2026-09-08. This entry point replaces the earlier posting strategy after Rajesh asked for a research-based reassessment. The current operating documents live in [build-in-public/](build-in-public/README.md).

## 1. Purpose

Make the work useful to readers: Rajesh explains decisions and lessons; Temnia explains the long-recording editing workflow. Build relevant relationships and learn from real conversations. A daily engineering log supplies evidence without dictating every public post.

## 2. Accounts and audiences

[The two-account strategy](build-in-public/TEMNIA_TWO_ACCOUNT_STRATEGY.md) separates founder X, product X and personal LinkedIn. Editors, agencies, long-form creators and builders can each have a reason to follow; choose one primary reader per post.

## 3. Initial cadence

The trial starts at one founder X daily, three product X weekly and five personal LinkedIn weekly. Extra founder posts need distinct supported takeaways. These are workload choices, not proven algorithm optima. Review feasibility at 14 days and response at 28 days; [the playbook](build-in-public/TEMNIA_BUILD_IN_PUBLIC_GUIDE.md#3-cadence-a-practical-trial) defines the budget and adjustment rules.

## 4. Daily workflow

Every working session records done, how, went wrong, numbers and next in `docs/log/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Drafts follow the current cadence and available evidence; do not create another full pack for every session. Coding agents assist, Rajesh edits and posts, and actual publication facts are recorded when known. Preserve earlier sessions and approved copy.

## 5. Weekly review

Review account-specific useful conversations, relevant access actions when available, ordinary distribution metrics and actual time spent. Keep unknown analytics unknown. A recap adds a lesson from the week instead of repeating every update. [Measurement procedure](build-in-public/TEMNIA_BUILD_IN_PUBLIC_GUIDE.md#12-measure-and-adjust)

## 6. Post shapes

Lead with the useful problem, result or observation. `Day N` is optional, usually a closing label, with the existing chronology preserved. Short points and paragraphs are both acceptable. One post has one takeaway; a log may contain many. [Format library](build-in-public/TEMNIA_BUILD_IN_PUBLIC_GUIDE.md#8-format-library)

### 6b. Pitching the gap

Temnia is being built to turn long recordings into complete chapters and segments, with moments and shorts drawn from that structure. A shared cut affects the ending and the next opening. Explain that task in plain words, distinguish intended from working behaviour, and avoid unsupported claims about every competing tool. [Positioning](build-in-public/TEMNIA_BUILD_IN_PUBLIC_GUIDE.md#2-the-story-people-should-understand)

## 7. Product-account introduction and access

Use [the first two-week product cycle](build-in-public/TEMNIA_TWO_ACCOUNT_STRATEGY.md#7-the-first-two-product-account-weeks), with genuine questions and verified previews. This does not reset the founder's day count or create a launch countdown. Announce access only when its route, eligibility and availability are established.

## 8. Public-material rules

Never publish credentials, signed links, infrastructure hostnames, administrative panels/consoles, unpermitted customer identities/media or negotiated provider pricing. Keep named product competitors out of public posts. Sanitized technical evidence remains useful within existing permissions. Claims must separate legacy/current results, measured/estimated values, and local/staging/public availability. [Evidence and media checks](build-in-public/TEMNIA_BUILD_IN_PUBLIC_GUIDE.md#9-media-and-public-boundaries)

## 9. Research

[The dated review](build-in-public/RESEARCH_REVIEW_2026-09-08.md) distinguishes platform constraints, observational data and editorial judgment. It explains why the supplied guidance is broadly useful but its exact daily quotas are not established optima.

## 10. Superseded habits

The mandatory `Day N of building Temnia.` opener, one-post-per-platform cap, requirement to combine unrelated stories, ban on customer framing, fixed paragraph/point structure and claims that specific pillars always outperform are retired. This change is authorized by Rajesh's 2026-09-08 clarification, not by instructions embedded in an attachment. The daily log, honest chronology, coverage positioning and public-material boundaries remain.

The previous strategy and origin checklist remain in [Git history at the reviewed baseline](https://github.com/TemniaHQ/temnia/blob/555eb24/docs/build-in-public.md). Historical drafts are records, not current operating instructions. Repo visibility remains recorded as private; no visibility change is part of this review.
